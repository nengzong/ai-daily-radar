  原始链接:https://fortune.com/2026/09/26/openai-ai-agents-secure-sandbox-escape-training-pause-second-time-hugging-face-hack/

- **Fireworks AI 发布 Ember-1:推理 token 减少 40%,同等质量下更省成本**
